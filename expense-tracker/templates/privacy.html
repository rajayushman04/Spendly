Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect:

  • Account details, such as your name, email address and password (stored in hashed form).
  • Expense data you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to operate Spendly: to let you sign in, record and review your expenses, and show you summaries of your spending. We do not sell your personal data or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in the application's database and is accessible only to your account. We take reasonable measures to protect it, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You can ask us to delete your account and associated data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when pages load. We do not share your expense data with any third party.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want your data removed, please contact the Spendly team at support@spendly.example.
